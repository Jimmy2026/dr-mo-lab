Principal Investigator

Dr. Moses Olayemi

Assistant Professor of Engineering Pathways · Gallogly College of Engineering · University of Oklahoma

Dr. Moses Olayemi (Dr. Mo) is a scholar at the intersection of STEM education, culturally relevant pedagogy, and socioeconomic development. Originally from Nigeria, he holds a B.Sc. in Chemical Engineering from the University of Lagos and a Ph.D. in Engineering Education from Purdue University, where he was awarded the Bilsland Dissertation Fellowship. His work has been recognized internationally — his paper on culturally relevant engineering education in Nigeria won the ASEE International Division's Best DEI Paper in 2023. At OU, he teaches Introduction to Engineering Thinking and is known as one of the college's most student-centered instructors.
