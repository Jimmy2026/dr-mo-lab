DRMO Lab is a research group dedicated to developing researchers and advancing multidisciplinary approaches to engineering education and outreach. We work across disciplines, institutions, and communities to create research that is responsive to real-world needs while expanding opportunities for students and researchers to engage in meaningful scholarship. Through collaboration and outreach, we seek to connect research with the people and communities it is meant to serve.
